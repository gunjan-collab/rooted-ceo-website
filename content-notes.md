# Content Notes from Business Coaching Frameworks Document

## New Four Pillars (Gunjan's Own — no external names)

### 1. People & Team
- Build high-performing teams using Working Genius assessments
- Delegation mastery — hire to buy back your time
- Assign work based on natural strengths
- Reduce friction, accelerate results
- "Your business is capped at the speed of your delegation"

### 2. Communication & Leadership
- Lead with clarity, empowerment, and authenticity
- 1-3-1 decision framework (one problem, three solutions, one recommendation)
- Fractional coaching model — embedded leadership support
- Shift from clinician identity to CEO identity
- Emotional energy as leadership currency

### 3. Systems & Operations
- Build a business that runs without you
- The Three Ps: Playbooks, People, Platforms
- SOPs, documented processes, predictable outcomes
- Compound Effect — small consistent actions = radical results
- Operational independence

### 4. Growth & Harmony
- Sustainable scaling with work-family harmony at centre
- From chaos builder to empire builder
- Predictable revenue without burnout
- MaxOut in ALL life areas — not just business
- "Be The Exception" — choose greatness over mediocrity

## 12-Month Implementation Journey

### Months 1-3: Foundation
- Assess current identity and mindset
- Audit time and delegate low-value tasks
- Establish daily compound habits
- Clarity session to identify obstacles

### Months 4-6: Systems
- Clarify your offer and eliminate low-margin services
- Build predictable growth engine
- Implement team strengths assessments
- Practice consistent growth actions

### Months 7-9: Leadership
- Install decision frameworks for your team
- Develop leadership support systems
- Serve team and clients deeply
- Clarify your unique positioning

### Months 10-12: Scale & Culture
- Build culture systems that outlast you
- Business runs without constant involvement
- Achieve harmony across all life areas
- Become the exception in your market

## Key Themes to Weave In
- Systems over hustle
- Identity transformation (clinician → CEO)
- Delegation & team building
- Sustainable growth (not burnout)
- Purpose & service
- Compound Effect philosophy
