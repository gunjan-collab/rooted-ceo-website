- Coaching program for healthcare professionals (physiotherapists, clinic owners)
- Teal + sandy gold on white, beach-inspired palette
- Key themes: growth, roots, harmony, ambition + relationships
- Logo: Symmetrical tree with teal branches and sandy gold roots

## Idea 1: "Coastal Organic" — Nature-Forward Warmth

**Design Movement:** Organic Modernism meets Coastal Living — inspired by Australian/California coastal design studios. Think Aesop meets wellness retreat branding.

**Core Principles:**
1. Organic shapes and flowing lines that mirror natural growth patterns (roots, branches, waves)
2. Generous white space that breathes like an open shoreline
3. Warm materiality — textures of sand, linen, and driftwood
4. Asymmetric layouts that feel curated, not templated

**Color Philosophy:**
- Primary teal (#1a7a7a) represents depth, trust, and the ocean — used for headings and CTAs
- Sandy gold (#c9a96e) represents warmth, value, and sunlit sand — used for accents and highlights
- Warm white (#faf8f5) background evokes beach sand, not sterile clinical white
- Deep charcoal (#2d3436) for body text — warm, not cold black
- Soft sage (#e8f0ec) for section alternation

**Layout Paradigm:** Flowing vertical narrative with staggered content blocks. Sections alternate between full-width immersive moments and contained asymmetric grids. Content flows like a river — left-aligned hero, right-aligned about, centered pillars — creating visual rhythm.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Subtle wave/organic dividers between sections (SVG curves, not straight lines)
2. Circular motifs echoing the logo's tree circle — used for avatars, icons, and decorative elements
3. Fine gold accent lines and borders that add premium warmth

**Interaction Philosophy:** Gentle, nature-inspired — elements fade and rise like tides. Hover states use subtle warmth shifts (teal deepens, gold glows). Nothing jarring; everything feels like a gentle unfolding.

**Animation:** Scroll-triggered fade-up reveals with staggered delays. Pillar cards gently scale on hover. The hero tagline types or fades in word-by-word. Smooth parallax on background textures.

**Typography System:**
- Display: "Playfair Display" (serif) — elegant, rooted, premium feel for headings
- Body: "DM Sans" — clean, modern, highly readable sans-serif
- Accent: Gold-colored small caps for labels and section markers

## Idea 2: "Structured Growth" — Editorial Precision

**Design Movement:** Swiss Editorial Design meets Wellness Branding — inspired by Kinfolk magazine and modern healthcare branding. Clean grids with soul.

**Core Principles:**
1. Strong typographic hierarchy that commands attention and builds trust
2. Modular grid system with intentional breaks for emphasis
3. Photography-forward design with teal/gold duotone overlays
4. Restrained decoration — let content and whitespace do the work

**Color Philosophy:**
- Deep teal (#0d6b6e) as the authority color — headers, nav, key CTAs
- Warm gold (#d4a853) as the aspiration color — pricing highlights, badges, accents
- Pure warm white (#fffdf9) for breathing room
- Muted teal tint (#f0f7f7) for alternating sections
- Rich dark (#1a2e35) for footer and contrast sections

**Layout Paradigm:** Magazine-style editorial grid with 12-column structure. Content blocks snap to grid but occasionally break out for dramatic effect. Large type treatments anchor each section. Two-column layouts for content-heavy sections, full-bleed for emotional moments.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Oversized section numbers (01, 02, 03...) in light gold as navigational anchors
2. Horizontal rule accents in gold that separate and connect content
3. Teal pill-shaped badges and tags for categorization

**Interaction Philosophy:** Precise and intentional — elements slide in from their grid position. Hover reveals additional information through subtle expansion. CTAs have confident, decisive animations.

**Animation:** Content blocks slide in from left/right based on grid position. Section numbers count up on scroll. Cards have a subtle lift-and-shadow on hover. Smooth scroll-snap between major sections.

**Typography System:**
- Display: "Sora" — geometric, modern, confident for headings
- Body: "Source Serif 4" — readable serif that adds warmth and credibility to body text
- Mono: Section labels in a clean monospace for editorial feel

## Idea 3: "Rooted Luxe" — Premium Wellness Retreat

**Design Movement:** Luxury Wellness meets Boutique Hotel Branding — inspired by Four Seasons wellness programs and high-end retreat centers. Aspirational yet approachable.

**Core Principles:**
1. Cinematic visual storytelling with large imagery and atmospheric depth
2. Layered depth through overlapping elements, shadows, and translucency
3. Premium restraint — fewer elements, each with more impact
4. Warm luxury — gold accents feel earned, not gaudy

**Color Philosophy:**
- Rich teal (#1b7a7a) as the grounding color — represents depth and expertise
- Burnished gold (#c4a265) as the premium accent — represents value and transformation
- Cream (#fdf9f3) as the canvas — warm, inviting, luxurious
- Deep forest (#1a3a3a) for dramatic contrast sections (about, footer)
- Soft blush (#f5ede4) for gentle warmth in alternating sections

**Layout Paradigm:** Cinematic full-width sections with contained content. Hero is full-viewport with layered elements. Content sections use generous padding (120px+) creating a sense of spaciousness. Asymmetric image-text pairs where images bleed to edges while text is contained.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Frosted glass/translucent cards that float over subtle background textures
2. Gold leaf-like decorative accents — thin lines, small botanical illustrations
3. Large circular image crops echoing the logo's circular tree motif

**Interaction Philosophy:** Luxurious and deliberate — elements reveal themselves with cinematic timing. Parallax depth on images. Hover states feel like touching silk — smooth color transitions with subtle scale.

**Animation:** Cinematic scroll reveals with opacity and transform. Hero elements layer in sequentially. Cards have a glass-morphism hover effect. Smooth, eased transitions everywhere (cubic-bezier curves, not linear).

**Typography System:**
- Display: "Cormorant Garamond" — luxurious, high-fashion serif for headings
- Body: "Outfit" — modern, clean geometric sans-serif for readability
- Accent: Spaced uppercase Outfit for section labels and CTAs

## Selected Approach: Idea 1 — "Coastal Organic"

This approach best aligns with the brand's beach-inspired palette, the organic tree logo, and the warm-yet-professional tone needed for healthcare professionals. The flowing layouts and organic shapes reinforce the "rooted" metaphor while the coastal warmth makes the brand approachable and aspirational.
